What can a complete renovation include?

The scope can include removing your existing bathroom, preparing surfaces, plumbing alterations, tiling, fitting sanitaryware and finishing. Your quotation will set out what’s included, what you supply and any exclusions.

Can you fit a bathroom I’ve already bought?

Tell us which products you’ve chosen and send any plans or product details. We’ll discuss fitting requirements and confirm the work we can quote for.

How much will my bathroom renovation cost?

Cost depends on room size, condition, layout changes, fittings and finishes. Send photos and your postcode so we can discuss the project and the next steps for a quotation.

How long will the work take?

The schedule depends on preparation, plumbing changes, tiling and the availability of your chosen fittings. We discuss timing and access to your bathroom before work begins.

Which parts of London do you cover?

We welcome enquiries across London. Send your postcode with your project details so we can confirm availability for your address.
