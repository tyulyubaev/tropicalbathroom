Your enquiry details.

This page describes how this website’s enquiry links work. For questions, email quote@tropicalbathroom.com.

What happens when you fill in the form?

The form prepares your name, postcode, phone number and project details in your browser. It does not submit them to a website database. Selecting WhatsApp or email opens that service with your message prepared. You choose whether to send it.

When you send a message

Details you send are used to discuss your renovation enquiry and any resulting work. Please include only information needed to discuss the project. WhatsApp and your email provider handle messages under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and external services

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include tracking scripts. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives technical request information such as your IP address. Your browser may cache website resources.

Questions about your information

Contact quote@tropicalbathroom.com to ask about information you have sent, request a correction or request deletion. We will discuss your request and explain any information that needs to be retained in connection with your project.
